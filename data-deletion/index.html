Remove data at the source.

Armory Relay has no developer-operated account database. Your Riot session and store cache are held on the Android device and can be removed locally.

Deletion methods Inside the app Android settings Riot account
Method A

Inside the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Clear cache to remove cached store and catalogue data.
  3. Select Sign out to remove the encrypted Riot session, cookies and store data associated with that session.
Method B

Android settings

  1. Open Android Settings and choose Apps.
  2. Select Armory Relay: Daily Shop.
  3. Choose Storage & cache, then Clear storage.

Uninstalling the app also removes its local application data from the device.

External account

Deleting a Riot account

These steps do not delete your Riot account or data retained by Riot Games. Armory Relay cannot process that request. Use Riot’s official account deletion process.
